Cosmo Card Engine

Watch. Recognize. Design. Reveal.

The system is not a screenshot dump. Cosmo identifies moments with emotional, visual or cultural importance, removes interface clutter, composes a real card design, records provenance and binds the finished set to the media token and its first owner.

01

Moment intelligence

Scene changes, dialogue, music cues, objects, characters and viewer reactions help Cosmo choose frames worth collecting.

02

Unlimited art direction

Every frame receives a design suited to its world: racing, fantasy, cartoons, music, science fiction, history or something entirely new.

03

Personalized scarcity

The set is generated for one viewing session and ledgered with its recipe, owner, source token and edition data.

04

Three Star Coin unlock

The viewer can peek at concealed cards, then spend three Star Coins to reveal and move the complete set into the wallet.

05

Wallet custody

Cards can be hidden, displayed, organized with the movie or song, or prepared for transfer inside the collector community.

06

Contextual commerce

Real products that naturally fit a scene can appear beside—not on top of—the collectible experience.

Example experience

R.A.D. becomes a living collectible and shopping world.

While a viewer watches the cult BMX movie, Cosmo can recognize bikes, helmets, racing gear, locations, tricks, music and important story beats.

  • Create a one-owner R.A.D.-inspired card set from selected viewing moments.
  • Offer real BMX bikes, protective gear and apparel that match the scene context.
  • Keep advertising separate from card ownership and provenance.
  • Attach the set to the movie’s Infinity token as a collectible data package.
  • Reveal the set at sharing or the end credits, then unlock it for three Star Coins.
Infinity token attachment

One media token, many connected artifacts.

Movies and songs become hubs rather than flat catalog entries. Each Infinity token can carry the media record, rights and payout references, Cosmo observations, advertising matches and viewer-owned card sets.

RecordPurposeOwner / authority
Media tokenCanonical movie, episode or song identityStarQuest ledger
Scene recipeFrame time, crop, style seed and generation recordCosmo engine
Card set tokenSet identity, edition, card count and reveal stateViewer wallet
Provenance trailCreation, unlock, custody and authorized transfersAppend-only ledger
Commerce matchesRelevant products, availability and disclosuresAdvertiser/catalog service
Royalty referencesRights-holder and payout routing dataRoyalty ledger
Value is discovered, not promised. The ledger can prove origin, scarcity and ownership history. Collector demand determines whether a card has resale value. Production must also use licensed media imagery and clear commercial permissions before public sale or transfer.
Build target

Cosmo should make the reveal feel magical—and the record trustworthy.

Capture safely

Use authorized playback sources, scene timestamps, rights rules and privacy-preserving viewer signals.

Generate reproducibly

Store prompts, design parameters, hashes and source references so every minted result can be audited.

Transfer deliberately

Separate locked, revealed, wallet-held, displayed, listed and transferred states. Never move ownership on a visual button alone.
