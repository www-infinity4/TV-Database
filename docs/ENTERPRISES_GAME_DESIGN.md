# Enterprises — connected research game concept

**Status:** concept and future build roadmap  
**Home:** StarQuest Game Deck / TV-Database  
**Working title:** *Enterprises*

## Premise

*Enterprises* is an original science-and-research game built around fictionalized, stylized versions of Elon Musk and Sam Altman. Their appearance is a creative depiction and does not imply endorsement, participation, sponsorship, or approval by either person or their companies.

The characters can serve two roles depending on the selected game format:

- **Mission leaders in role-playing and enterprise modes.** They recruit unusually capable players, assign large research missions, and evaluate completed systems.
- **Playable characters in action-platform levels.** Each character has a distinct toolset that interacts with scientific machinery, energy networks, compute systems, research objects, and environmental obstacles.

The objective is not merely to finish levels quickly. The game searches for productive researchers, explainers, engineers, curators, collaborators, and people who discover unusual but useful connections.

## Playable characters

### Musk — thermal engineering

Musk carries a fictional experimental thermal tool inspired by a flamethrower. It is a game mechanic, not a real construction guide.

Possible functions and upgrades:

- remove ice, debris, or damaged material;
- activate thermal engines and hydrogen test systems;
- change material state for manufacturing puzzles;
- focus or widen the heat field;
- ignite controlled propulsion for temporary flight;
- power smelting, repair, or energy-transfer equipment.

### Altman — software-defined magnetic orb

Altman controls an AI-managed magnetic defense orb. The orb consumes power and must be upgraded, recharged, and used efficiently.

Possible functions and upgrades:

- create a temporary defensive field;
- deflect projectiles;
- attract research fragments and conductive objects;
- manipulate remote switches;
- collect ions;
- create short-lived magnetic platforms;
- cover both characters during cooperative play;
- optimize machines and reduce simulated compute cost.

The characters are strongest when switched or coordinated. Musk changes physical systems; Altman changes information, compute, fields, and control systems.

## Core power bars

Each mission contains four connected progress bars:

1. **Energy** — route and conserve usable energy.
2. **Compute** — complete the task with fewer processing cycles.
3. **Research** — discover evidence, formulas, vocabulary, and experimental clues.
4. **Enterprise** — combine the discoveries into a working system or publishable artifact.

Filling all bars unlocks the most efficient known solution for that playthrough. Efficiency is measured by useful output, evidence quality, energy consumption, compute consumption, and reuse—not simply elapsed time.

## Learning through action

The educational layer should not resemble worksheets placed on top of a game. Before a mission, the player supplies a small configuration:

- ten words or concepts;
- a field of study;
- several quick research choices;
- an intended project or question.

Those choices generate the tools, obstacles, research fragments, vocabulary, and artifact goals inside the level.

Examples:

- running starts or advances an AI research process;
- blocks reveal evidence, excerpts, variables, or source trails;
- power-ups represent tools created from the player's selected ideas;
- swimming levels explore coolant, data, or fluid systems;
- flying exposes relationships between separated concepts;
- portals open new scientific disciplines or historical periods;
- enemies represent errors, unsupported claims, missing evidence, or system failures;
- bosses require several discoveries to be combined into one solution.

Short spoken-style callouts appear during action, similar to a racing-game driver speaking during a race:

> Pressure increased—rebalance the hydrogen flow.

> The calculation works, but the compute path can be reduced.

> Formula fragment unlocked. Test it at the gateway.

## Evidence labels

Every scientific excerpt or generated artifact must display one of three evidence states:

- **Established science** — supported by cited, reproducible sources.
- **Active hypothesis** — a proposal or interpretation that has not been established.
- **Enterprise fiction** — a speculative game mechanism used for storytelling or simulation.

Hydrogen gateways can therefore exist as a central fictional mission while known hydrogen chemistry, energy, pressure, magnetism, and ion-transfer principles remain clearly distinguishable from speculation.

## First world: Hydrogen Gateway Laboratory

The first complete prototype should prove the full loop:

1. enter a damaged research complex;
2. select or import ten research concepts;
3. route hydrogen through controlled valves and chambers;
4. collect formula and evidence fragments;
5. use Altman's orb to manipulate ion and magnetic systems;
6. use Musk's thermal tool to activate or repair physical machinery;
7. complete swimming, flying, racing, and platform sections;
8. optimize the balance between energy and compute;
9. activate the fictional gateway after all four bars are filled;
10. publish the player's research artifact.

The generated artifact should contain cited established science, clearly labeled hypotheses, simulation results, player decisions, contributor attribution, and the final game design.

## Connected multiplayer research universe

Players participate in one shared research universe without needing to be online simultaneously.

Supported collaboration modes:

- synchronized cooperative levels;
- opt-in live voice or text research rooms;
- asynchronous books, schematics, datasets, formulas, experiments, and inventions;
- invitations when another player's work matches the current problem;
- imports from other players with a visible attribution chain;
- cooperative machines whose bars are filled by several specialties.

Example notification:

> Samantha completed an Astrophysics Field Book. Three findings may improve your ion-transfer model.

The player may inspect the book, import a relevant finding, invite Samantha into a mission, or continue independently. Shared artifacts must be opt-in; private research and voice activity remain private.

## Talent and productivity scoring

The leaderboard should not be dominated by time played or platforming speed. Rankings are multidimensional:

| Signal | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Research quality | Evidence and conclusions are well supported |
| Language precision | Vocabulary and explanations improve the work |
| Compute efficiency | Strong output uses fewer simulated resources |
| Energy efficiency | Systems produce more useful work with less energy |
| Originality | The player discovers new, useful combinations |
| Reuse | Other players successfully apply the artifact |
| Collaboration | Contributions improve a shared mission |
| Correction | The player finds and repairs mistakes |
| Teaching value | Explanations help other players understand |
| Completion | Fragments become a usable artifact |
| Verified impact | A later simulation or mission demonstrates improvement |

Separate boards can recognize researchers, engineers, curators, explainers, collaborators, efficient system designers, and original thinkers.

## Game formats

The same research and attribution systems can drive several formats:

- original side-scrolling action platformer;
- role-playing enterprise missions;
- racing and vehicle engineering;
- laboratory and factory simulation;
- cooperative research expeditions;
- time-travel and historical science missions;
- player-generated research worlds.

The project may use familiar platform-game fundamentals—running, jumping, blocks, power-ups, swimming, flight, and portals—but must use original characters, environments, artwork, music, narrative, and level designs rather than copying protected game assets.

## StarQuest Game Deck connection

The deployed Game Deck currently provides:

- verified Internet Archive game-source discovery;
- system-specific emulator selection;
- full-screen and minimized play modes;
- adaptive controller presentation;
- browser-local game inventory;
- prototype game activity receipts;
- a 0.05 qualifying-play receipt after five minutes;
- a separate 0.05 confirmed-share receipt;
- one StarCoin after twenty 0.05 receipts.

Automatic ROM admission is limited to items with recorded homebrew, public-domain, Creative Commons, freeware, open-source, or explicit redistribution evidence. Internet Archive availability alone is retained as source evidence but is not recorded as proof that a publisher authorized redistribution.

Publisher obligations use `pending_contract_settlement` until a rights owner, contract rate, settlement account, approval, and transaction reference exist.

## Video and game reward target

The intended unified fractional contract is:

| Activity | Viewer receipt |
| --- | ---: |
| Video reaches 95% watched | 0.05 StarCoin |
| Confirmed video share | 0.05 StarCoin |
| Qualifying game session | 0.05 StarCoin |
| Confirmed game share | 0.05 StarCoin |

The browser-side game receipt exists. The Cloudflare ledger still uses the older integer ten-share schema, so cross-device fractional rewards and video 95%-watch settlement require a coordinated database and worker migration before they can be described as server-authoritative.

## Delivery roadmap

### Phase 1 — playable original prototype

- original character sprites and laboratory environment;
- platform movement and mobile controls;
- Musk/Altman character switching;
- thermal tool and magnetic orb;
- four power bars;
- one Hydrogen Gateway Laboratory level;
- research excerpt and evidence labeling;
- end-of-level research artifact.

### Phase 2 — research generation

- ten-word mission configuration;
- source retrieval and citation;
- established/hypothesis/fiction classification;
- compute and energy scoring;
- player artifact storage and attribution.

### Phase 3 — connected universe

- shared artifact catalog;
- contributor identities and permissions;
- synchronous and asynchronous collaboration;
- multidimensional leaderboards;
- invitations based on demonstrated work.

### Phase 4 — durable economy

- fractional StarCoin server migration;
- idempotent watch, play, and share events;
- verified rights-holder registry;
- contract-based accrual;
- protected settlement review;
- transaction references and reversals.
