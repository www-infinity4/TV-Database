## Architecture decision

Infinity and Omni are encoded as direction modes, not physical claims:

- **Infinity** is unbounded directed extent along a chosen vector.
- **Omni** is multidimensional coverage over the active canvas.
- **Convergent sink** directs many boundary streams toward a focal core.
- **Source / sink** combines outward starburst expansion with inward gravity.

The visual engine consumes deterministic geometry events. It does not receive
filesystem, credential, merge, payment, attack, or device-control authority.
Multi-profile AI roles are `resilience`, `resource_planning`, `builder`,
`reviewer`, and `researcher`; adversarial behavior is confined to fixtures and
simulations.

The Actuator is a guarded proposal service. It emits a typed patch request;
the repository gateway checks path allowlists and secret boundaries, creates a
non-default branch, records a diff, runs tests and security checks, and opens a
PR. Promotion remains a separate owner-reviewed action.

Unique `/k`, `/z`, Base, Octave2, and custom symbolic formats remain distinct.
Round four adds mappings and provenance without renaming or collapsing them.
